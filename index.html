Projects

Project × stage grid

Every project against every pipeline stage in one view. Green = done (with the date range and whether it blew its SLA at the time), the highlighted column = where the project is right now (days in stage vs. SLA, colored if it's approaching or over), grey = not reached yet. Click a project or cell to open the full detail view.

Hawk-eye

Every project's pipeline stages plotted on one shared calendar — solid blocks are stages already lived through (or being lived through now), colored by SLA status and sized to their real dates; the dashed chips after them are what's still ahead, grouped into one compact strip (not date-plotted — just planned order). A blue "Today" line runs through every row. Click a row or block to open the full detail view, scrolled to the Stage-gate timeline.

Status history

Every status change (On Track ↔ At Risk / Delay ↔ Critical) and every go-live / milestone date shift, across every project, newest first — with dates, what changed, and the why. Click a project name to open its full detail view. Use the Project filter above to see just one project's story: how it got delayed, when its timeline shifted, and why.

Dependencies

Every open cross-team dependency, when it's actually needed by, and how urgent it is — rolled up two ways: by project (what's each project waiting on) and by team (what's on each team's plate across every project).
